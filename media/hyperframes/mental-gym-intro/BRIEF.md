# Mental Gym — Choose Your Path

## Purpose

Explain Mental Gym's learning system and introduce the four six-month AI engineering curricula from the homepage.

## Delivery

- 24 seconds
- 1920 × 1080, 16:9
- Silent motion graphic
- Deterministic HyperFrames render
- Native MP4 playback inside an accessible homepage dialog
- First frame must work as the poster and thumbnail

## Audience

Engineers using Mental Gym for structured AI engineering study, implementation practice, interview preparation, and evidence-building.

## Message

Choose one path, work within a fixed weekly budget, and build evidence rather than collecting disconnected course material.

## Visual direction

Reuse Mental Gym's restrained editorial style:

- Warm ivory background
- Charcoal typography
- Muted gold accent
- Glass-like cards and fine borders
- Serif display type with compact sans-serif labels
- Slow, deliberate motion rather than flashy effects

## Constraints

- No generated media or paid services
- No external runtime assets
- No autoplay with sound
- No claims of personalized progress
- No account, synchronization, or model API dependency
